# Needs theory research boundary

This directory is a quarantined research notebook. It is not product content and it is not a production data source.

## Hard boundary

- Nothing in this directory may be imported, compiled, copied, generated, or loaded by the application, backend, tests, production content pipeline, or site build.
- Do not add this directory to a workspace package, Vite input, script input, content compiler, runtime fetch, public asset path, or deployment step.
- Do not treat statements here as approved allneeds doctrine or as approved Need-page copy.
- Do not modify production content, application code, generated data, or canonical editorial data while working only on this research project.
- Any future proposal to move a conclusion into the product requires Nat's explicit approval and must then follow the repository's ordinary psychological-content, evidence-review, audit, and Bedrock ownership rules.

## Epistemic discipline

- NVC is a source of hypotheses, observations, practices, and normative commitments, not an unquestionable authority.
- Preserve authorship and provenance. Distinguish Nat's own claims from agent-generated synthesis, quotations, and later interpretation.
- Material of mixed or uncertain authorship must be labeled mixed/uncertain; do not silently attribute it to Nat.
- Use claim labels where practical: definition, observation, phenomenon candidate, hypothesis, postulate, measurement claim, intervention claim, and normative commitment.
- Record contrary evidence, rival explanations, uncertainty, and revision history.
- A claim's presence in this directory means only that it is under consideration.
- Citations must be human-readable and directly reachable. Do not invent or infer bibliographic details.

## Working method

- Append dated entries to `research-log.md`.
- Register source materials in `sources/source-register.md`.
- Put provisional theoretical syntheses in `working-theory/`.
- Preserve superseded drafts when their history matters; mark their status rather than rewriting history.
- Prefer small, reviewable commits confined to this directory.
