# Needs Theory Research Project

Status: **private intellectual scaffolding inside the repository; completely disconnected from the site**

This project treats Nonviolent Communication (NVC) as a historically developed prototheory: a body of observations, hypotheses, normative commitments, and practices that may contain genuine insights about human needs, values, motivation, affect, and action. The aim is not to preserve every NVC claim. The aim is to reconstruct whatever survives conceptual analysis and systematic evidence review into a clear, testable theory.

The long-range aspiration is a sincere academic paper suitable for journal submission. Nothing here is yet a manuscript, settled ontology, approved allneeds content, or production authority.

## Current research question

What must “Need,” “value,” “motive,” “feeling,” “goal,” and “strategy” mean for an NVC-descended theory to be conceptually coherent, empirically testable, and capable of revising or rejecting its own inherited claims?

## Current methodological commitment

NVC supplies candidates, not conclusions. Every descriptive proposition must be capable of being narrowed, reclassified, or rejected. Normative commitments must be identified separately from causal and empirical claims.
