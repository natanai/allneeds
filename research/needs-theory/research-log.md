## 2026-08-29 — Project record established

### Purpose

Create a durable, running record for the intellectual development of a testable theory descended from NVC, with a long-range aspiration of producing a sincere academic paper and later applying sufficiently supported conclusions to allneeds.

### Boundary decision

The work is deliberately siloed from production. No application, compiler, data source, test, build configuration, route, or deployment setting was changed. The folder's local `AGENTS.md` prohibits future agents from connecting it to the site without Nat's explicit approval and the repository's normal review process.

### Source added

Registered Nat's earlier document, “Needs are Gift - AI Copy.” Nat warned that it contains additions from a previous agent that are not necessarily Nat's definitive thoughts. Its propositions were extracted as candidates, not endorsements.

### Provisional foundation carried forward

Recorded Theory Charter v0.1 as agent synthesis. It treats NVC as a prototheory and distinguishes concerns, Needs, Basic Needs, values, motives, goals, strategies, and feelings. It also records claim types, preliminary postulates, criteria for basicness, and risky predictions.

### Central tensions identified from the earlier draft

- universality versus individual, developmental, and cultural variation;
- ever-presence versus current activation;
- motivational contribution versus determination of all behavior;
- feelings as meaningful evidence versus one-to-one Need decoding;
- non-responsibility for another's feelings versus interpersonal causation and moral responsibility;
- equal legitimacy of Needs versus practical scarcity and trade-offs;
- “Needs are gifts” as metaphysics, norm, phenomenological practice, or intervention;
- requests versus demands under power and threatened consequences;
- “serving life” relative to an explicit standard of functioning.
