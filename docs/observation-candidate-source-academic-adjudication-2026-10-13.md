## Evaluation rubric

1. **Identity and access.** Verify the original publisher or primary host; disclose when only abstract, accepted manuscript, preprint or metadata available. Use **original publisher article URL**, not a DOI redirect, as the main link (bare DOI may be kept in separate bibliography metadata).
2. **Claim-to-source fit.** Prefer evidence for the specific concept the guide actually teaches; decline papers that only vaguely resemble the process.
3. **Study design and statistical inference.** Distinguish randomized comparison, quasi-experiment, cross-sectional association, psychometrics, systematic review, narrative review and theoretical framework. Reconcile denominators, attrition, controls, measurement and important null results.
4. **External and construct validity.** Distinguish observed communication, self-rated empathy, neurological activation, clinical outcomes, and simulated tasks. Most studies are NOT direct tests of AllNeeds.
5. **Counterevidence / safety / inclusion.** Preserve negative findings and do not imply personal memories are illegitimate, responsibility for wrongdoing should be softened, emotion naming always makes feelings better, or psychological needs can be deterministically inferred.

**Statuses:** **RETAIN** = credible *for a narrow, stated claim* in the proposed guide/source library, still awaiting separate user approval and final wording verification. **HOLD** = relevant but insufficiently checked or too context-specific for present public use; seek full text / better comparator / language review or keep only as internal record. **EXCLUDE** = not suitable as user-facing Observation research justification; preserve bibliographic provenance and perhaps use in a separate internal research program.

**Placement** refers to one or more of the existing `src/data/observationGuide.json` research sections or a proposed distinct **Direct NVC / Journal / Strategies research subsection** where an existing Observation-only section cannot honestly contain the subject.

**Important caveat:** This was a targeted multi-round literature search, not a complete systematic review. A source marked RETAIN is never called clinically validated. Publisher-host links were independently checked where obtainable; some editorial entries point to the known journal landing route, and entries without a verified original publisher page (N10, N13, M06) explicitly use the author/institutional source rather than fabricating a publisher page.

## How I would rebuild the guide from these decisions

**A. Observation method (core guide).** Explain the practical distinction between observed action and inferred meaning using M01, M02, M10 and C09. Never call first-person reflection scientifically objective or suggest that less judgmental words invalidate personal experience. Include M09 for how wording is perceived in hypothetical conflict, but clearly label its outcomes.

**B. Research behind the Feeling step (linked, not an Observation claim).** Lead with M07, C03 (abstract-level with Korean-language caveat), C01/C02 (sequence-dependent negative effects), M08 (physiology not self-report), C11/C12 (association, not treatment); M06 is a neural experiment, not a clinical claim. Make Feeling selection optional, especially when labels do not feel helpful; these studies do not justify forcing a rigid sequence.

**C. Research behind Needs.** M16 defines the boundaries of studied SDT autonomy/competence/relatedness; C06/C07 show modest outcomes for autonomy-supportive interventions in health domains. Do NOT claim these sources validate the entire NVC Need vocabulary or a specific Feeling-to-Need mapping. M17 population-specific data is held.

**D. Strategies and journal (separately labeled adjacent-resource sections).** C08 supports if-then action planning only when users actually form cue+response plans; C04 and C05 together show uncertain, small/heterogeneous emotional-disclosure effects, not that saving an AllNeeds journal entry clinically improves health. M05 can explain that intensive concreteness interventions are different from a brief event description.

**E. What is known about NVC itself.** A transparent short 'NVC studies' section should state that most packages involved hours/days of instruction, structured role play, feedback and social practice. N01 maps scarcity, N02 shows a small self-rated outcome with major attrition, N11 shows short-term self-report gains, N06 shows suggestive workplace findings with serious bias, N15 shows striking immediate simulated educational outcomes but with unregistered design/unequal practice. These are NOT additive proofs of AllNeeds efficacy. N03 and several clinical studies held for fuller review.

**F. Methods/source library.** M13 and N04/N05 belong under rigor and tools rather than therapeutic promises. Avoid overloading the general user guide with M14 or memory-misinformation M15. Keep all excluded sources in the internal evidence archive for provenance, not remove them from history.

## Unverified clinical claims — reject without qualification

- 'The Observation → Feeling → Need → Strategy sequence is scientifically or clinically validated.'
- 'Separating observation from evaluation increases factual accuracy or prevents conflict.'
- 'Naming any feeling will make that person calmer or facilitate every later coping strategy.'
- 'Selecting a Need identifies the correct psychological cause of a feeling.'
- 'The AllNeeds journal/strategy inventory treats PTSD, depression, anxiety, or interpersonal conflict.'
- 'All distress can be improved through voluntary language changes' or 'abstract interpretations are inherently invalid.'
