> Status: approved by the user on 2026-08-27 and implemented in `src/data/editorialCatalog.json`.

This is a wording-only refinement pass over the already-approved Connection, Support, Safety, and Understanding packages. It applies the standing Human Editorial Style rule without reopening their research conclusions, Need boundaries, citation sets, strategy provenance, or Need associations.

The production catalog is the canonical public wording. Where an older audit document quotes a superseded sentence from one of these four Needs, this record and the production catalog supersede only that quoted wording. The earlier source-role notes and evidence decisions remain in force.

## Criteria used

A sentence was changed only when it met at least one approved criterion:

- internal audit/reviewer language leaking into public Details;
- generic signposting or a `Taken together` synthesis that added no information;
- stock contrast scaffolding or claim-then-walk-back structure;
- unexplained research or clinical terminology where plain language preserved the meaning;
- automatic comprehensiveness or list padding;
- repetitive polished closing prose;
- the explicitly rejected `draw us to` / `drawn to` wording.

Legitimate lists, technical distinctions, and protected user wording were left alone when they served the content.
