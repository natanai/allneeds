## Decision framework

- **RETAIN (26)** = scholarly source worth integrating into a carefully qualified evidence guide *after the user approves source and exact sentence separately*. It is not a declaration that a therapy or the website has demonstrated effectiveness.
- **HOLD (13)** = relevant, but available source depth, design uncertainties, effect sizes, comparators, population match, or redundant claims prevent immediate site-facing approval.
- **EXCLUDE (9)** = not suitable to cite as support in *this public Observation research guide* due to weak applicability, redundancy, risk of reader misinterpretation, preliminary publication, or lower-quality alternatives. Archival record remains preserved.

The site makes a model commitment to **Observation → Feeling → Need → Strategy**, with valid motivations, non-deterministic Feeling–Need inference and Strategy-level rather than Need-level conflicts. Research may examine parts of the model; it must **not** be presented as trial validation of the whole sequence.

All sources assessed for bibliographic plausibility, population, design, causal inference, direction of null/contrary results, actual task versus product task, source-to-claim specificity, risk of credibility harm, journal-vs-manuscript status, and fit to the nine-section `site-test` Observation guide. Prior Rounds 1–4 include full-text or abstract-level access labels. This pass cross-checked important publisher pages and corrected interpretation where possible, but **did not newly obtain and complete formal full-text risk-of-bias scoring for every paper**.

**Link requirement:** Direct original publisher article webpages are the preferred displayed links. No generic `doi.org` article-link redirects. A verifiable original university record or national academic catalogue is used when the publisher page could not be established. Some publisher host links are structurally consistent and matched to published DOI metadata but still require final click/reachability checks (especially APA). Preserve bare DOI identifiers in previous historical logs as metadata only.

## Decision summary

**26 retain / 13 hold / 9 exclude from reader-facing guide.** These are editorial recommendations, never user acceptances. No publication or deployment authorization.

### Required sections

- **Observation methods:** M01; measurement discipline and its limits M13 in a compact methods note.
- **Language, framing and inference:** M02, C09, M10, with M05 clearly identified as separate clinical concreteness training rather than evidence for the writing interface.
- **Feelings and affect labeling:** M06, M07, M08, C01, C02; explicitly display conflicting/negative results. C14 is conceptual background.
- **Interpersonal communication:** M09, M11, with non-deterministic perception language and preservation of justified responsibility.
- **Needs:** M16 plus carefully qualified intervention context C06 and C07.
- **Strategy:** C08 as evidence for implementation intentions, not passive suggestion browsing.
- **Journal:** paired C04 and C05 rather than cherry-picking favorable writing effects.
- **NVC direct research (separate research-status section):** N01, N02, N06, N10 (qualitative only), N11, N13; N15 and other held interventions must not be selectively portrayed as confirmed success.
- **Recall/credibility:** Do not use the excluded eyewitness misinformation or isolated word memory studies as if they assess the truth or reliability of an individual's personal observation.
- **Experimental gap:** No clinical trial shown here validates the four-step NVC procedure as implemented in this app; no NVC component dismantling experiment identified by targeted search.

## Component-level tests and counterevidence (C01–C14)

| ID | Clinical-academic proposal | Position in guide | Why the source survives / fails scrutiny |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C01 | **RETAIN** | Feeling / interactions and opposing evidence | Two experiments show emotion naming before reappraisal or mindful acceptance can weaken short-term negative-affect regulation. **Limitation:** Unpleasant image tasks rather than voluntary emotional reflection; does not show Feelings identification is globally harmful. |
| C02 | **RETAIN** | Feeling / replication and sequencing | Two newer experiments replicated a naming-before-reappraisal impediment, without clear delayed advantage. **Limitation:** Laboratory negative-image experiment, not an NVC path test. Effects depend on temporal sequence, instructions and affect measure. |
| C03 | **HOLD** | Feeling / Korean systematic synthesis | 43-study systematic review appears to identify physiological benefits alongside mixed self-report effects, addressing language/publication selection. **Limitation:** Full Korean text, methods and risk-of-bias work not yet reviewed; avoid representing narrative direction as pooled clinical efficacy. |
| C04 | **RETAIN** | Journal / disclosure evidence and uncertainty | Meta-analysis of 146 disclosure experiments suggested a small overall mean effect (r about .075). **Limitation:** Multiple outcomes, small effect, heterogeneity; full bias/moderator tables still required; disclosure exercises differ from AllNeeds journaling. |
| C05 | **RETAIN** | Journal / contrasting disclosure synthesis | 61-trial systematic review found no clear improvements for objective physical health or most outcomes; directly challenges unqualified journaling promises. **Limitation:** Older trial set, often poor study quality and different outcomes from C04; cannot claim writing never helps either. |
| C06 | **RETAIN** | Needs / supporting motivation | Meta-analysis of 73 SDT-informed experimental health interventions provides broader context for autonomy/competence/relatedness support. **Limitation:** Interventions alter settings and support, not selection of one personal NVC Need or AllNeeds screen. Distinguish broad constructs from site labels. |
| C07 | **RETAIN** | Needs / randomized interventions | RCT-only SDT meta-analysis (65 contrasts) found small average health behavior benefit, constraining enthusiastic efficacy extrapolation. **Limitation:** May share primary trials with C06; do not count as independent evidence or infer that named Needs cause change. |
| C08 | **RETAIN** | Strategy / if-then planning (explicitly adjacent) | Meta-analysis of 94 implementation-intention tests supports forming a context-specific action plan. **Limitation:** Choosing a Strategy or request is not equivalent to an if-then plan; do not suggest a passive strategy browser has demonstrated outcomes. |
| C09 | **RETAIN** | Language / interpretation caveat | Five experiments show concrete-versus-abstract descriptions shift beliefs about biological and psychological causes. **Limitation:** Crucial limit: concrete framing can itself shape and sometimes increase psychological attributions, so 'concrete is unbiased' is false. |
| C10 | **HOLD** | Communication / concrete criticism and reception | Two small intergroup-criticism experiments suggest concreteness may reduce perceived prejudice/hostility in specific out-group contexts. **Limitation:** Original full measures, controls and moderator effects need checking; cannot generalize to individual conflict or abusive dynamics. |
| C11 | **HOLD** | Feeling / granularity background | 40-study review finds links between lower emotion differentiation and more adolescent depressive symptoms; anxiety and mediation results less clear. **Limitation:** Not emotion-vocabulary training, no diagnosed younger adolescent samples, limited generalizability; tenuous clinical inference. |
| C12 | **HOLD** | Feeling / granularity transdiagnostic map | Scoping review of 98 articles/114 studies maps emotion granularity across psychological health contexts. **Limitation:** Largely observational, possible overlap with C11 and no direct testing of naming feelings to treat symptoms. |
| C13 | **EXCLUDE** | Research archive / provisional only | Submitted preprint reviews mixed emotion differentiation–regulation associations; useful warning sign. **Limitation:** Not final peer-reviewed source; completeness and analytic robustness unverified; stronger published reviews can anchor guide. |
| C14 | **RETAIN** | Feeling / conceptual distinctions | Influential process-model review distinguishes regulation identification, selection and implementation. **Limitation:** Conceptual synthesis not a clinical trial. Useful to distinguish ideas, never to claim AllNeeds sequence is validated. |

## Product-safety and editorial recommendations

- Do not label user statements inaccurate merely because they contain interpretations or emotional language; the tool is an organizing aid, not an adjudicator or credibility detector.
- Avoid implying emotions uniquely identify particular Needs. AllNeeds Need terms are supportive framework categories, not universally established diagnostic constructs.
- Do not claim fewer arguments, better mental health, validated emotion regulation, improved memory accuracy or superior conflict resolution from routine use of AllNeeds until studied in an appropriate trial.
- Do not weaken reporting of coercion, aggression or harm merely to remove an alleged 'judgment'; responsibility may be accurate and ethically significant.
- Expose methodological limitations with each studied claim, with full citations in a slim reader-facing view rather than a dense stack of redundant source cards.
