## What a recommendation means

- **RETAIN:** eligible for a narrow proposed research role, not a judgment that every claim is checked or that a study validates allneeds.
- **HOLD:** no public claim proposed until the missing methods, numerical results or other stated problems are resolved.
- **EXCLUDE:** unsuitable for this guide's current claims; preserve its identity and previous appraisal in the research archive.

Totals: **52 unique records; 34 RETAIN, 4 HOLD, 14 EXCLUDE.** The 34 retained records comprise 18 proposed core citations and 16 optional research candidates. Records and review counts must not be presented as independent trials; syntheses can overlap. These are editorial recommendations, not a formal certainty score or user approvals.
