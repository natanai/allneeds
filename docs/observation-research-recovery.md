## Why this is being restored

The earlier `natanai/nvc-app` Observation guide combined an academic-style abstract, **eight desktop sections and 18 linked references** with an additional short “Why try this?” explanation that linked references **[19] and [20]**. The React rebuild (2026-08-24) switched all viewports to the shorter mobile guidance and removed the four linked introductory research sources. The 2026-10-11 consolidation eliminated the unused desktop guide data and reduced public references to five.

The site-test restoration returns to the **full subject breadth** (research on observable description, abstract/concrete language, affect labeling, attribution, conflict, behavioral measurement, memory and needs) rather than bringing back the old source claims verbatim. One canonical dataset presents the same topics on every device. Desktop uses a topic index; mobile uses a native topic picker rather than three lines under every accordion arrow.

The public preview currently displays **nine topics and 15 traceable research and methods sources**. In every finding, `method`, `result`, `meaning` and `limits` are separate authored fields. The page makes no claim that the allneeds Quick Check, the Observation→Feeling→Need sequence or NVC has been clinically validated by these studies.

## Review states / publication discipline

- **Bibliographic/abstract check:** identified the article, topic, design and important limitations from a publisher, PubMed, or publicly available article. This is all that most restored source summaries currently claim; it is **not** a full replication or clinical-certainty stamp.
- **Official practice guidance:** the IRIS Center resource on ABC data collection is instructional, not a primary trial; the U.S. Surgeon General advisory is public-health synthesis, not a trial of Observation writing.
- **Provisional or incorrectly matched:** original references [1], [3], [6], [9], [11], [14], [16], [17], [18] remain visible in this **historical ledger**, not as evidentiary proof in public copy.
- **No status-by-count claims:** the site should not display “15 clinically verified citations.” The reference titles are publications with varying research designs and applicability. Additional papers should only be promoted when the specific claim and limitation are written down.
- **Next review passes:** read the full methods and results for every active study, replace any mismatched abstract-level paraphrase, distinguish clinical relevance from outcomes, check all links, and assess direct interventions on observation/NVC if available. A systematic search strategy is still needed before saying this is a comprehensive review of the field.
- **Preservation:** the Oct 2025 guide and the Nov 2025 “Why try this?” state remain retrievable by commit in the original repository; this record explicitly retains all 20 source identities as working research history.
